The company’s road

A reason to hold.
A company to return to.

The campaign follows a small company across three chapters and twelve encounters. Its choices carry into the next battle and the journey home.

  1. Hold Lantern Gate

    Keep the crossing open while the families escape. Select a companion, choose an order and its target, then let the enemies answer. The company shares its orders.

  2. Return to camp

    Keep earned gold and equipment, hear from the company, and prepare for the next fight. Lysa is looking for her brother Ivo; the road ahead follows the missing wages.

  3. Choose what to protect

    Escort a convoy, recover a relic and face the winter siege. Reinforcements, wards and evacuation choices change later encounters and what the company remembers.

The war chest

What you pack becomes what you can command.

Weapons, shields and relics compete for a thirty-cell chest. Their shapes and neighbours change the company’s options.

Earn a Storm rune at Lantern Gate, then pack it against a weapon’s edge. Its +2 damage can turn Rowan’s eight-damage Cleave into ten: enough to finish a ten-health raider.
