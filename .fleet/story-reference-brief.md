# Story presentation reference brief — October 2, 2026

Read original product descriptions and narrative-design documentation, not a claim that screenshots or commercial outcomes prove our game's quality.

- [The Banner Saga, Stoic](https://stoicstudio.com/banner-saga): a tactical campaign frames decisions as responsibilities carried along a journey. Transfer the connection between the road and consequences; keep War Chest's own company, wages, three fronts and adult flat art. Do not copy Viking identity, caravan systems, art or dialogue.
- [Pyre, Supergiant](https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/pyre/): the party shares a concrete journey toward freedom. Transfer a goal understood through the people travelling with you. Do not copy its competition, setting, visual language or cast.
- [Wildermyth's official event documentation](https://wildermyth.com/wiki/Event_Types): arrival, tactical, victory and recruitment scenes connect character development to specific combat situations. Transfer short context at those transitions and consequences that change play. War Chest remains an authored deterministic road, not a procedural story generator or permanent-death system.

These are narrative principles inferred from the linked primary sources. Native composition is tested in our own three rendered direction systems using War Chest content and existing assets.

Anti-reference: a contract dashboard with numbered fights and a paragraph pasted above it. That repeats the current weakness: the player learns what pays out before understanding whom the company is helping. Also reject lengthy lore before the first action and unlabelled choices that only change a later line of dialogue.

Quality bar: immediate named stakes, short readable speech, visible party presence, one clear next task, honest choice consequences, a finite road, and remembered actions at home. Each direction must show a scene and the next preparation context at native supported sizes; a polished title screen alone is insufficient.
