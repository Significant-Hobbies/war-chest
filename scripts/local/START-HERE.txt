WAR CHEST — LOCAL PLAY BUILD

Double-click Play War Chest.command to continue your company. A genuinely new
company begins The Winter Wages story; existing companies keep their modes.
Double-click Practice War Chest.command for a fresh story session that never reads
or writes your campaign save or settings. Practice progress is not saved.

Keep this whole folder together. The runtime and both Banner & Steel artwork
images are included. No editor, export templates, downloads or account needed.
This local bundle uses the existing Godot editor binary as a game runner;
it is not a separately exported, signed or notarized War Chest application.

HOW TO PLAY
All companions deploy. Bottom portraits or 1–5 select whose cards you command;
switching costs no orders. Choose a card, then click its target.
Space: end turn. Z: undo your last order. T/F: preview/confirm a target.
Lead Rowan, Lysa and Fen through twelve encounters. Hear the company before
each march and make three choices that change later battles and the homecoming.
The first victory awards a storm rune. Pack it beside a weapon, then test its
+2 command damage in battle two. Objectives include rescuing Ivo, escorting
supplies, carrying a seal and bringing the pay chest home under fire.
Pack equipment before battle; stored items have no effect.
Earn gold in battles and upgrade gear. Merrin joins free after the Sunken
Reliquary and deploys next battle; pack his staff for Storm.
Company levels advance after Gate, Convoy, Watchfires, Iron Oath and Winterwatch,
up to level 6. The road names your next milestone. Individual mastery grows
separately at 2, 5 and 9 victories. Talon opens after the story ending.
Existing companies retain their original missions, quests and recruitment.
No real purchases.
Menu contains help, sound and reduced-motion settings.

SAVES

~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/War Chest/iron-and-ember-v2.json
The authored story uses a separate company:
~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/War Chest/winter-wages-story-v1.json
Menu lets an existing company begin or resume the story in that separate slot.
No save is included in this bundle. Existing malformed saves are preserved;
the game reports that saving is disabled instead of replacing them.
