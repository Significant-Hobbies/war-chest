WAR CHEST — LOCAL PLAY BUILD

Double-click Play War Chest.command to continue your campaign.
Double-click Practice War Chest.command for a fresh session that never reads
or writes your campaign save or settings. Practice progress is not saved.

Keep this whole folder together. The runtime and both Banner & Steel artwork
images are included. No editor, export templates, downloads or account needed.
This local bundle uses the existing Godot editor binary as a game runner;
it is not a separately exported, signed or notarized War Chest application.

HOW TO PLAY
Click a hero (or press 1–5), choose a card, then click a target.
Space: end turn. Z: undo your last order. T/F: preview/confirm a target.
The first battle teaches commands and awards a storm rune. Pack it beside a
weapon, then test its +2 command damage in battle two. Guided opening introduces
side quests/contracts/banners afterward; Explore without guidance opens them
at their existing level/story requirements. Old saves retain existing access.
Pack equipment before battle; stored items have no effect.
Earn gold in battles, recruit Merrin and upgrade gear. No real purchases.
Menu contains help, sound and reduced-motion settings.

SAVES
Campaign uses the existing location:
~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/War Chest/iron-and-ember-v2.json
No save is included in this bundle. Existing malformed saves are preserved;
the game reports that saving is disabled instead of replacing them.

DIAGNOSTICS
Verify Local Game.command checks bundle hashes, the earned twelve-encounter
journey, native control logic, onboarding and motion logic without player data.
Any engine error makes verification fail, even when gameplay assertions pass.
Logs are kept in this folder's logs directory, one folder per session.

Native window rendering and the reported background crash have NOT been
requalified by this build. Headless checks do not prove graphical stability.
If a macOS security warning appears, do not disable security protections.
Do not delete your save to troubleshoot a launch failure.

The restricted build host could not validate the installed runtime's macOS
signature. The copied executable matches both the installed executable and
the retained download byte-for-byte. This is not a successful Gatekeeper or
notarization check; a clean native launch still needs confirmation.
