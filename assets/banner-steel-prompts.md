## Lantern Gate background

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image is the approved Banner & Steel art reference. Produce a production-ready BACKGROUND ONLY for its 2D strategy game, 16:10 landscape. Preserve the flat simplified cartoon drawing, warm limestone, olive trees, navy shadows, ochre torchlight, three broad stone bridges approaching a gate. REMOVE ALL UI, ALL TEXT, ALL TROOPS, WOLF, PORTRAITS, health bars, markers, numbers, dots, selection outlines, command cards. The image must contain only environment. Recompose the THREE playable bridges almost parallel, with clear broad walkable centers at 25%, 50%, 75% of canvas width, from 25% through 72% of canvas height; each must be wide enough for three small soldiers abreast. Keep upper 18% quieter castle wall suitable for live HUD overlay. Bottom 25% a quiet foreground stone terrace for live native command rail overlay. Keep only empty cloth banners without emblems or writing at the outer edges. Simple flat two-tone shapes, sparse contours, NO painterly stone texture, NO realistic lighting, no 3D render, no cute faces, no additional bridge. No labels, no baked shadow silhouettes.

## Company atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Approved image is STYLE reference only. Create a production game sprite atlas on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background with 12 isolated 2D full-body sprites arranged in exactly FOUR evenly spaced columns and THREE evenly spaced rows, equal cells, large transparent gutters, no text, no labels, no grid lines, no scenery or ground planes. Large landscape 4:3 canvas. Flat restrained hand-inked cartoon style matching the reference, two-tone color blocks, clean navy outlines, adult proportions (head one sixth of height), no chibi, no realism, no gradients, no photographic textures. Keep feet baseline aligned within each row, all whole figures within their cell including weapons. Each sprite small crisp silhouette with generous 12 percent cell padding. Row1 left to right: Rowan dark-haired veteran swordsman with navy cloak sword and kite shield; Lysa female olive-cloaked archer holding bow; Fen lean grey wolf side-view; Merrin older storm mage in muted blue robes holding staff. Row2 left to right: Talon tawny owl wings partly spread; wood-and-iron ballista; hostile brick-red cloaked skeleton spearman; hostile armored shield guard in brick-red surcoat. Row3 left to right: hostile hooded archer with brick-red cloak; hostile sapper with round powder satchel and hammer; enemy captain with tall steel helmet brick cape and sword; a freestanding olive company standard bearing a small pale sword symbol. Humanoids mostly three-quarter front view facing slightly left, readable when drawn at 80-140 pixels tall. Keep all twelve separate and fully visible. Genuine alpha transparency not a painted checkerboard.
